TensorOps gaps — BatchedMatMul is the most critical missing piece. Attention is [Batch, Heads, SeqLen, HeadDim] @ [Batch, Heads, HeadDim, SeqLen] — a 4D batched operation that the current 2D matmul can't express directly. Everything else (Subtract, Multiply, MaskedFill, ArgTopK, public Transpose) is straightforward to add. MatMulInner_AVX2 also still has a Fma.IsSupported branch inside it — that needs to split into two separate kernel methods.
Missing modules — EmbeddingTable (integer index → float vector) and RoPE are non-negotiable before the Model layer. Without RoPE, LLaMA-family models can't be built. Dropout, CrossEntropyLoss, and LogSoftmax are training-only so they can come in parallel with the training layer rather than blocking Data.
MoE — only Gather, Scatter, and TopK belong in Core. Everything else (expert dispatch, router logic) lives in the Model layer. Don't over-build Core for MoE now.
